A lone laborer arrives at a half built ship at midnight with one job: replace the broken planks. The work is simple, repetitive and grounding until it isn't. As exhaustion sets in, the ship begins to betray the sense, and what started as a mundane night shift becomes something quietly, deeply wrong.

Shutter

Shutter is a single-player psychological horror puzzle game where you play Ray Walkers, a detective moonlighting as a photo developer in a claustrophobic red-lit darkroom. Each night, anonymous murder polaroids arrive under the door, and you develop them by hand through chemical trays, then arrange them chronologically on a corkboard to solve the case. With no monsters or jumpscares, the dread comes purely from graphic imagery, minimal diegetic UI, and sound-driven atmosphere.
